# Demo film: FraudLens (3:49)

The film is built as code, so every cut, zoom and caption is reproducible:
- **Narration.** `video/script.json` holds the lines. `video/voice/make_voice.py` voices them with Gemini 2.5 Pro TTS (Charon), and `video/voice/qa_audio.py` checks every clip for dropped words.
- **Screen.** `video/capture/record.mjs` drives the real Command Center against TigerGraph Savanna with Playwright and records it at 2560×1440. It also logs the exact on-screen box of every element the film looks at.
- **Edit.** `video/src/` (Remotion) directs a virtual camera to those boxes, time-warps the live run onto the narration, and adds the motion-graphics scenes, captions and the music bed (Google Lyria, generated on our GCP project).

Render it with `cd video && npx remotion render FraudLensDemo out/fraudlens-demo.mp4`.
